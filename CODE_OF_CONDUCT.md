# Code of Conduct

Emberline welcomes people who want to build, play, teach, and learn together. Everyone participating in this project is expected to help keep its community respectful and constructive.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect across differences in identity, background, experience, and ability.
- Critique ideas and code without attacking the people behind them.
- Be patient with questions and explain disagreements in good faith.
- Keep feedback relevant to Emberline and its community.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, discrimination, insults, threats, or sustained personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or attention that is unwelcome.
- Publishing someone's private information without permission.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or repeated unwanted contact.

## Where this applies

This code applies in project spaces, including issues, pull requests, reviews, and project-related conversations, as well as when someone is representing the project publicly.

## Reporting and response

Report a concern privately by contacting a repository maintainer through GitHub. Do not post sensitive details in a public issue. For security vulnerabilities, follow [SECURITY.md](SECURITY.md).

Maintainers will review reports fairly and confidentially where possible. They may edit or remove content, close discussions, request corrective action, or restrict participation when needed to protect the community. Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith is not acceptable.
